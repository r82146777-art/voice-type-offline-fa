## Recognition/runtime evidence

- `com.k2fsa.sherpa.onnx` classes are referenced extensively.
- The DEX references `OfflineSpeechDenoiserConfig` and `OnlineSpeechDenoiserConfig`.
- The DEX references `VadModelConfig` and `sileroVadModelConfig`.
- The DEX references offline and online recognizer configuration types, including Whisper, Paraformer, Zipformer, SenseVoice, Moonshine and other sherpa-onnx model families.
- Actual bundled assets include `assets/asr/model.int8v2.onnx.xz`, `assets/asr/silero_vad.onnx.xz`, and a separate English Moonshine encoder/decoder/token set.
- Android speech service references include `android.speech.RecognitionService`, `android.speech.action.RECOGNIZE_SPEECH`, and `android.speech.extra.PARTIAL_RESULTS`.

## Why this matters

The APK contains recognizable app-specific classes in DEX, but the previous source archive did not yield their Java/smali source files. It is therefore not enough to edit only the recovered `BuildConfig.java` or framework files. The next technical step is to disassemble the original `classes.dex` correctly and inspect the implementations of `AsrEngine`, `VoiceImeService`, `SpeechListener`, and `ModelManager` before changing behavior.
